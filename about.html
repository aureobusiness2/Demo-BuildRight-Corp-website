Who We Are

From a Small Team to a Trusted Name

BuildRight Construction Corp. was founded in 2009 by Engr. Ramon Villanueva, a licensed civil engineer with over 20 years of fieldwork experience. Starting with a crew of eight and a single residential project in Quezon City, we grew steadily — project by project, referral by referral.

Today, we are a full-service general contractor with over 50 licensed engineers, architects, and skilled workers. We have completed more than 320 projects across Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Bulacan, Rizal, and Pampanga — from simple home renovations to multi-storey commercial buildings.

What has not changed since day one is our core commitment: deliver what we promise, charge what we quote, and build it to last. Every project carries our name — and we take that seriously.

"We do not just build structures. We build trust — with every truss, every column, every slab we pour."

— Engr. Ramon Villanueva, Founder & CEO
