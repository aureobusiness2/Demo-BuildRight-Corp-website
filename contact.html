Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to inquiries?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent matters, call us directly at +63 917 555 0192 — we are available Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you offer free site visits and quotations?

Yes — site visits and initial quotations are completely free of charge. We cover Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna, Bulacan, Rizal, and Pampanga. Just send us your project details and we will schedule a visit at your convenience.

What areas do you serve in the Philippines?

Our primary service areas are Metro Manila and nearby provinces — Cavite, Laguna, Bulacan, Rizal, and Pampanga. For projects outside these areas, please contact us and we can discuss feasibility on a case-by-case basis.

How long does it take to receive a detailed quote?

After an initial consultation and site visit, a detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with itemized costs in Philippine Peso is typically prepared within 5–7 business days depending on the scope of your project.

Can I use the contact form for project quotes?

For a general inquiry, yes — use the form on this page. For a more detailed project quote including budget range, project type, and file uploads, we recommend using our dedicated Get a Quote page instead.

Do you handle building permit processing?

Yes. Our Full Construction and Design & Build packages include permit processing with the LGU. Our team prepares all required engineering and architectural documents for building permit applications in your city or municipality.
